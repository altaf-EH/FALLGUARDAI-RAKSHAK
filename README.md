 **Next-Gen Autonomous AI Safety, Fall Detection & Emergency SOS Guardian System**

**FallGuard AI Rakshak** is a privacy-first, military-grade personal safety and emergency response application designed for women, elderly individuals, solo travelers, and students. It operates both online and offline to detect falls, dispatch instant emergency alerts, capture forensic evidence, and safeguard lives in critical situations.

## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### 🚨 1. Real-Time Autonomous SOS & Fall Detection
- **3D Inertial Fall Monitor:** Continuously monitors accelerometer and gyroscopic data to detect sudden impact, high-G falls, or prolonged immobility.
- **Smart 10-Second Countdown:** Gives you time to cancel false alarms with sound and haptic vibration feedback before automatically triggering emergency dispatches.
- **Shake-to-SOS (Stealth Mode):** Vigorously shake your phone (3 times) to silently dispatch emergency alerts without tipping off attackers.
- **Hardware Button Trigger:** Rapidly press physical volume keys to trigger instant SOS.
- **Voice-Activated SOS:** Listens for emergency keywords (e.g., *"Help"*, *"Bachao"*, *"Emergency"*) to trigger hands-free protection.

### 📍 2. Live GPS Tracking & Bilingual HUD
- **Real-Time Interactive HUD:** High-precision GPS tracking with pinpoint accuracy radius, altitude, and heading indicators.
- **100% Free & No API Key Needed:** Powered by high-speed OpenStreetMap and Leaflet engine.
- **Instant Google Maps Routing:** Single tap to navigate directly to the victim’s location.
- **Bilingual Clean Geocoding:** Live street address display in 100% pure English or pure Hindi without confusing mixed scripts.

### 📹 3. Forensic Evidence Blackbox (Camera & Audio Recording)
- **Automatic Stealth Capture:** Automatically records dual-angle camera snapshots and high-fidelity ambient audio clips during any emergency trigger.
- **Tamper-Proof Incident History:** Securely timestamps GPS coordinates, medical logs, and evidence photos in a local blackbox vault for legal and police forensics.

### 📞 4. Fake Escape Call (Tactical De-escalation)
- **Schedule Escape Calls:** Receive a realistic, full-screen incoming phone call (e.g., *"Papa Calling"*, *"Police Control"*, *"Brother"*) within 5 seconds or a custom timer.
- **Realistic Voice Playback:** Plays pre-recorded interactive dialogue through the speaker when answered, providing a realistic excuse to exit dangerous or uncomfortable situations safely.

### ⏱️ 5. Journey Safety Timer (Dead Man's Switch)
- Set a countdown timer when walking through deserted roads, taking late-night cabs, or traveling alone.
- If you don't check in or enter your safe PIN before the timer reaches zero, the SOS sequence is triggered automatically.

### 📡 6. Offline Bluetooth Mesh SOS & Low Battery Beacon
- **Zero-Internet Bluetooth Mesh:** Broadcasts emergency beacons directly to nearby devices over short-range radio signals when cellular networks are down.
- **Low-Battery Emergency Beacon:** Automatically saves your last known coordinates before the phone dies.

### 🏥 7. Offline Medical ID & Direct 112 Dispatch
- **Instant Emergency Access:** Displays blood group, allergies, emergency contacts, and vital medical conditions directly on the lock screen.
- **One-Tap Emergency Numbers:** Direct dialer integration for **112 (National Emergency)**, **100 (Police)**, **108 (Ambulance)**, and **1091 (Women Helpline)**.

## ⚙️ Essential Phone Settings for 24/7 Background Protection

Modern Android manufacturers (Xiaomi, Realme, Vivo, Samsung, OnePlus, OPPO) aggressively close background apps to save battery. To ensure Rakshak protects you 24/7 in the background:

### 1. Disable Battery Optimization (Critical)
- Go to **Phone Settings** ➔ **Apps** ➔ **FallGuard AI Rakshak**.
- Tap **Battery** (or Battery Usage) ➔ Select **"Unrestricted"** (or *"Don't Optimize"*).

### 2. Enable Auto-Start / Background Activity
- **Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO:** Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Manage Apps ➔ Rakshak ➔ Enable **"Autostart"**.
- **Realme / OPPO / Vivo:** Settings ➔ App Management ➔ Rakshak ➔ Enable **"Allow Background Activity"** and **"Auto-launch"**.
- **Samsung:** Settings ➔ Device Care ➔ Battery ➔ Background usage limits ➔ Add Rakshak to **"Never sleeping apps"**.

### 3. Lock App in Recent Apps Screen
- Open Rakshak ➔ Open the Android **Recent Apps (Multitasking)** screen.
- Long-press (or tap the lock icon) on the Rakshak card to **Lock** it in memory so it is never cleared when swiping away apps.
